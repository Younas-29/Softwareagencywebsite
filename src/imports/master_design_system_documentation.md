# 10. Motion System

## Motion Style
Subtle premium motion only.

---

## Allowed Animations
- Fade-up reveal
- Slight scale on hover
- Smooth section transitions
- Gradient movement
- Parallax (minimal)
- Floating cards
- Soft mouse interactions

---

## Avoid
- Excessive bouncing
- Flashy animations
- Neon effects
- Gaming transitions
- Aggressive parallax

# 11. Component Design Rules

## Buttons

### Primary Button
- Gradient blue/indigo
- Medium height
- Rounded-xl
- Soft glow on hover
- White text

### Secondary Button
- Transparent background
- Thin border
- Subtle hover fill

### Ghost Button
- Minimal hover background
- Used inside navbars/cards

---

## Cards

### Standard Card
- White background
- Thin border
- Soft shadow
- 24px radius

### Glass Card
- Semi-transparent
- Blur effect
- Soft border
- Used sparingly

### Bento Cards
- Large spacing
- Asymmetrical layouts
- Visual hierarchy

---

## Inputs
- Large comfortable height
- Soft gray borders
- Strong focus states
- Minimal styling

---
